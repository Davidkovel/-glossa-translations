# Versio Translations

## What it is
A translation and localization agency (working name Versio; formerly Glossa / Lexora). Written and certified translation, website and app localization, games and iGaming, audio and video (subtitles, voice-over, interpreting), editing and proofreading of human or AI drafts.

## Facts we may state (from the owner's earlier site)
- In translation since 2022 (4 years); 26,000+ projects delivered; 120+ languages.
- Every text passes three people: translator (native, knows the subject), editor (meaning, numbers, terms), native proofreader.
- About 2,000–2,500 words a day per linguist with all checks; urgent orders split across a team, same day possible.
- Certified translation of passports, diplomas, certificates with stamp and signature, standard or same-day.
- NDA on request; files deleted after delivery on request.
- Payment: card, bank transfer (EUR, USD, UAH), USDT, invoice for companies.
- −10% on the first order, applied automatically.
- Clients named on the old site: 1win, Parimatch, WhiteBIT, GGPro, Microsoft, Uber (cases: 1win EN→ES·PT·TR, Parimatch EN→UA·KZ, WhiteBIT EN→DE·PL·TR, Uber EN→UA·PL).
- Case scopes and all FAQ answers are the owner's own copy from the old site (lexora/index.html): 1win casino & sportsbook localization for local markets; Parimatch promo and app texts; WhiteBIT exchange interface, KYC and help center; Uber driver and rider texts; FAQ languages line and file-safety line.
- Price is given per request ("in 15 minutes"); no public price list.
- Contacts are placeholders until the owner registers them: hi@versio-translations.com, t.me/versio_translations.

## Audience
1. Marketing / product managers at iGaming, fintech and tech companies who need UI, promo and help-center text in many markets, fast and exact.
2. Private clients in Ukraine and abroad who need a passport, diploma or certificate translated and certified for an embassy, university or employer.
