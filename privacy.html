Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026.

What this site collects

Nothing is stored on this website. The order form runs in your browser: when you send it, your email app opens with the request already written, addressed to lingvotranslate.agency@gmail.com. We receive only what you send in that email or in Telegram.

What we do with your request

  • We use your name, email and phone only to reply to you and deliver the work.
  • Your files are shared only with the linguists assigned to your project.
  • On request we sign an NDA and delete your files after delivery.
  • We never sell or pass your data to third parties for marketing.

Cookies

We use only browser storage that remembers your chosen language and that you closed the cookie notice. No tracking or advertising cookies are set without your consent.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it: write to lingvotranslate.agency@gmail.com.
