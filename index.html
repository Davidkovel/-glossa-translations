Quick answers

How much does a translation cost?

It depends on the language pair, the length of the text and the deadline. Send us the text or describe it, and you get a fixed price within 15 minutes. The first order is 10% cheaper.

Which file formats do you accept?

Word, PDF, Excel, PowerPoint, text files, subtitles (SRT, VTT) and photos or scans. We keep the layout and formatting of the original.

How fast can you translate?

About 2,000–2,500 words a day per linguist with all three checks. Urgent orders are split across a team and can be ready the same day.

Can you certify documents?

Yes: passports, diplomas and certificates with stamp and signature, standard or same-day.

Which languages?

More than 120, including all EU languages, Turkish, Arabic, Japanese, Korean and Chinese.

Is my file safe?

We sign an NDA on request. Only your project's linguists see the files, and we delete them after delivery if you ask.

What if the embassy or court rejects the translation?

We redo it free and as fast as possible. Three people check every text, so it rarely happens.

Why not just a cheaper freelancer?

A freelancer works alone: nobody checks, nobody replaces them if they get sick. You get a team, a fixed deadline and a guarantee.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer (EUR, USD) or USDT. Companies can pay by invoice.
