Quick answers

How fast can you translate?

About 2,000–2,500 words a day per linguist with all three checks. Urgent orders are split across a team and can be ready in 24 hours.

Which languages?

More than 120: all EU languages, Ukrainian, Turkish, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Latin American Spanish and Portuguese and more.

Can you certify documents?

Yes: passports, diplomas and certificates with stamp and signature for official use.

Is my file safe?

We sign an NDA on request. Only your project's linguists see the files, and we delete them after delivery if you ask.

How do I pay?

Card, bank transfer (EUR, USD, UAH) or USDT. Companies can pay by invoice.
